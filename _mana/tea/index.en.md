# The Epic of Alexander (Ultimate)

Mana DC follows [Lily Doll's TEA strat](https://jp.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/character/34120564/blog/5274750/).
This is Nukemaru's strategy for TEA, but skipping Jagd Dolls in P1.

- [**Living Liquid**](01_living_liquid): Doll Skip (JP-style)
- [**Limit Cut**](02a_limit_cut): 1211
- [**Brute Justice + Cruise Chaser**](02b_bjcc): Nukemaru
- [**Alexander Prime**](03_alex_prime): 09STOP Wormhole (Korean sim strat- this
  is also called "Onyxia" by NA/EU)
- [**Perfect Alexander**](04_perfect_alex)

### BiS Notes

- Any gear at or above **i595** will have their substats capped.
- Relic weapons will have their substats capped at **184**.
- The lowest potions you can use and still get maximum benefits are **HQ Grade
  6 Tinctures**.
